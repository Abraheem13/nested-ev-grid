We presented a three-level nested controller for EV charging coordination in
which each level acts at its own timescale: an hourly price corridor, a
\SI{15}{min} dispatch policy that learns a residual on a deadline-aware
cheapest-slot plan, and a non-parametric layer that resolves price acceptance
every \SI{15}{min} and corrects voltage every \SI{60}{s} with inverter reactive
power. On two IEEE
feeders with AC power flow and real price, load and session data, the
controller kept the voltage floor without violations with accurate load
